A compact arcade garden about risk, rhythm, and banking a perfect harvest. Move the lantern with WASD or arrow keys, dash with Space, and collect glowing fruit while the orchard's sentry vines wake up. Collect every fruit (four in the first garden, rising to eight in the last), then enter the moon gate. Bank all five gardens to win. Each garden adds a faster sentry vine; touching a vine costs a heart. Hold Space to dash while stamina lasts, release it to recharge. Press M to toggle sound, and Enter or click to restart after a win or loss.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080/
```

The game is static and offline: no packages, APIs, fonts, images, or network requests are needed.
